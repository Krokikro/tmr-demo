By filling in the form on the website krokikro.github.io/tmr-demo (the “Website”) and ticking the consent box, I freely, of my own will and in my own interest give specific, informed, conscious and unambiguous consent to Limited Liability Company “Treydmedresurs” (TIN 9726115287, OGRN 1267700201460, address: premises 1/10, bldg. 12, 26 Varshavskoye Highway, Nagorny Municipal District, Moscow, 117105, Russia; the “Operator”) to process my personal data on the following terms.

1. Personal data

Name; phone number with country code; e-mail address. Also data transmitted automatically when the form is submitted: IP address, date and time the form was filled in, page address and language version of the Website, referral parameters (UTM tags), browser and device information.

2. Purposes

  • adding me to the early access list for the continuous glucose monitoring system;
  • informing me about the start of sales, terms for the first registered users and the possibility of taking part in clinical trials;
  • responding to my request.

3. Operations with personal data

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access) to persons engaged by the Operator to process data, blocking, deletion, destruction.

4. Processing methods

Mixed processing — both automated and non-automated. Personal data is stored in databases located in the Russian Federation. No cross-border transfer takes place.

5. Persons who may be engaged to process data

  • [hosting provider with a data centre in Russia: name, TIN and address — to be added before launch] — data storage on servers in the Russian Federation;

6. Term of consent

This consent is valid until the purposes are achieved, but no longer than 3 years from the date it was given, or until it is withdrawn.

7. Withdrawal of consent

I may withdraw this consent by sending a notice to [corporate e-mail address — to be added before launch] or by post to the Operator’s address. After receiving the withdrawal, the Operator stops processing and destroys my personal data within 30 days, unless federal law requires the data to be kept.

8. Confirmations

  • I am at least 18 years old. I am providing my own data.
  • I have read the Operator’s Privacy Policy. This consent is a separate document and is not part of the Policy.
