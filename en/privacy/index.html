1. General provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes how Limited Liability Company “Treydmedresurs” (the “Operator”) processes and protects the personal data of visitors to the website krokikro.github.io/tmr-demo (the “Website”).

1.2. The Policy is drawn up in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” of 27 July 2006 (the “Personal Data Law”) and sets out the Operator’s policy on personal data processing (Article 18.1(2) of the Personal Data Law). The Policy is publicly available on the Website.

1.3. The Policy applies to all personal data the Operator receives through the Website. The Website does not collect data for any purposes other than those stated in the Policy.

1.4. Operator details:

  • full name: Limited Liability Company “Treydmedresurs”;

2. Definitions

2.1. Personal data means any information relating directly or indirectly to an identified or identifiable individual (data subject).

2.2. Processing means any operation or set of operations performed on personal data, with or without automation: collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access), depersonalisation, blocking, deletion, destruction.

2.3. User means an individual who visits the Website or fills in a form on it.

2.4. Early access list means the list of Users who have submitted a request on the Website to receive information about the launch of the continuous glucose monitoring system and priority access to it.

2.5. Cookies are small pieces of data that the Website stores in the User’s browser.

2.6. Other terms have the meaning given to them by the Personal Data Law.

3. What personal data we process

3.1. Data the User provides in the request form: name; phone number with country code; e-mail address; consent to receive project news, if the User has ticked that box.

3.2. Data transmitted automatically when the form is submitted or the Website is visited: IP address; date and time of the request; page address and language version; referral parameters (UTM tags); browser and device information; cookie data and data of the Yandex Metrica web analytics service.

3.3. The Operator does not request or process special categories of personal data through the Website, including health data, or biometric data. Please do not enter such information in the form.

3.4. The Operator does not verify the accuracy of the data provided and assumes that Users provide their own accurate data.

5. Processing procedure and conditions

5.1. The Operator performs the following operations with personal data: collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access) to persons engaged to process data on the Operator’s behalf, blocking, deletion, destruction.

5.2. Processing is mixed — both automated and non-automated.

5.3. Recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification and retrieval of personal data of citizens of the Russian Federation are performed using databases located in the Russian Federation (Article 18(5) of the Personal Data Law).

5.4. The Operator does not transfer personal data across borders.

5.5. The Operator may engage other persons to process personal data under a contract obliging them to keep the data confidential and comply with the Personal Data Law (Article 6(3)). Such persons are:

  • [hosting provider with a data centre in Russia: name, TIN and address — to be added before launch] — data storage on servers in the Russian Federation;

5.6. The Operator does not sell personal data and does not disclose it to third parties without the User’s consent, except as required by the laws of the Russian Federation.

5.7. Once the purposes are achieved, the consent period expires or consent is withdrawn, the Operator stops processing and destroys the personal data within 30 days, unless federal law provides otherwise.

6. Cookies and web analytics

6.1. The Website uses technical cookies required for it to work (for example, to remember the selected language) and analytical cookies of the Yandex Metrica service.

6.2. Yandex Metrica helps us understand how visitors use the Website. Recording of data entered into form fields is disabled in the service.

6.3. Users can disable or delete cookies in their browser settings. Some Website features may not work properly afterwards.

7. User rights

7.1. The User has the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of their personal data (Article 14(7) of the Personal Data Law);
  • demand that personal data be clarified, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or not necessary for the stated purpose;
  • withdraw consent to personal data processing and consent to receive news;
  • demand that processing be stopped;
  • appeal against the Operator’s actions or omissions to Roskomnadzor or in court.

7.2. To exercise these rights, the User sends a request to [corporate e-mail address — to be added before launch] or by post to the Operator’s address marked “For the person responsible for personal data processing”. The request must contain the User’s name, contact details for the reply and information confirming the User’s relationship with the Operator (for example, the e-mail address or phone number given in the request and the approximate date of the request).

7.3. The Operator replies within 10 working days of receiving the request. This period may be extended by no more than 5 working days by a reasoned notice.

7.4. A demand to stop processing is fulfilled within 10 working days of receipt (extendable by no more than 5 working days by a reasoned notice). If consent is withdrawn, the Operator stops processing and destroys the data within 30 days, unless the law requires the data to be kept.

8. Data protection

8.1. The Operator takes the legal, organisational and technical measures provided for by Articles 18.1 and 19 of the Personal Data Law, including:

  • appointing a person responsible for organising personal data processing;
  • adopting internal rules on personal data processing and protection;
  • limiting access to personal data to employees who need it for their duties;
  • transmitting data from the Website only over a secure connection (HTTPS);
  • storing data on servers in the Russian Federation, keeping backups and protecting access with passwords;
  • assessing possible harm to data subjects and the effectiveness of the measures taken.

8.2. If an unlawful or accidental transfer of personal data affecting Users’ rights is detected, the Operator notifies Roskomnadzor within 24 hours and reports the results of its internal investigation within 72 hours (Article 21(3.1) of the Personal Data Law).

9. Minors

9.1. The Website is not intended for persons under 18. If a parent or legal guardian of a child submits a request, they provide their own contact details.

10. Final provisions

10.1. The Operator may amend the Policy. A new version takes effect upon publication on the Website unless it states otherwise. The current version is always available on this page.

10.2. The Policy is drawn up in Russian. If a translation differs from the Russian version, the Russian version applies.
